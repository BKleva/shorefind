Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Review Collector ("we," "our," or "the Service") helps businesses ask their customers for feedback and Google reviews by email and SMS. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Business owner account information — the email address and password used to sign up, plus business name, Google Place ID, and an optional logo.
  • Customer contact information — the name, email address, and/or phone number a business owner enters into their own account in order to send a review request. This information is provided directly by the business, not collected by us from any other source.
  • Message and response data — when a customer opens a review request and submits a star rating or comment, we store that rating/comment so the business owner can see it.

How we use this information

We use customer contact information solely to deliver the review-request message the business owner sends (by email and/or SMS) and to route the customer's response back to that business. We do not use this information for our own marketing, and we do not sell it.

SMS / mobile information

We do not share or sell mobile phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers collected through this Service are used only to send the review-request text message on behalf of the business that entered them, and for standard delivery/compliance handling by our messaging provider (Twilio).

Message frequency varies (typically one message per customer interaction). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to a message at any time to opt out of further messages, or reply HELP for help.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers required to operate the Service: our database/hosting provider (Supabase), our email provider (Resend), and our SMS provider (Twilio). None of these providers are permitted to use the data for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention

Customer contact information and message/response history are retained for as long as the business owner's account remains active, or until the business owner deletes the record.

Your choices

Customers can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. Business owners can delete customer records from their own dashboard at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the business you received a message from, or to ben@shoreworksnj.com.
