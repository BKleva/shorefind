Our Story

Four Decades of
Dressing Men Well

Lou Marchiano Owner & Founder

Since 1984, Lou Marchiano For Men has been Northfield's destination for men who want to look and feel their best. We're a true full service clothier — not a chain, not a discount rack, but a shop built on knowledge, fit, and personal attention.

From suits and tuxedos for the big day to the shirts, shoes, and accessories you reach for every day, we carry pieces chosen with care. Every purchase includes complimentary in-store alterations, because a great garment isn't finished until it fits you exactly right.
