How It Works

Three steps to
more reviews.

No apps for your customers to download, no accounts for them to create — just a text or email.

1

Why It Works

Why not just send
the Google link directly?

You can — and Review Collector still uses your real Google review link under the hood. The difference is the pre-check: customers who had a bad experience get a private feedback form instead of a public review box, so you find out about problems directly instead of reading them on Google first.
