About

South Jersey roots.

Ben Kleva

I'm a South Jersey native with over seven years working as a data analyst and data engineer, including in the government contracting space where I helped develop CRM tools for government technology resellers. That work taught me how to build systems that are clean, functional, and actually used by the people they're built for.

Working at that level — where data pipelines, user workflows, and business outcomes had to align — gave me a deep understanding of what makes a digital product succeed and what makes it fall flat.

Now I'm bringing those skills home. Local restaurants, shops, contractors, and service providers deserve the same quality of digital presence that big companies take for granted. Shoreworks Web Solutions is how I make that happen.
