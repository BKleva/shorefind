South Jersey's Premier Local Catalog

Built for the shore's hardest workers

Shoreworks Catalog is a niche spotlight advertising catalog purpose-built for the businesses that make Atlantic, Ocean, and Cape May counties what they are — places people love, return to, and call home.

Our mission

We created Shoreworks because we believe the businesses driving South Jersey's communities deserve more than a generic listing buried in a search engine. The family restaurants, the trusted service companies, the local shops, the outdoor outfitters — they've earned real visibility.

This is a curated, hand-selected catalog. Every business in Shoreworks represents the best of what these three counties have to offer. Our Spotlight tier puts exceptional businesses front and center, giving them the premium presence they've worked hard for.

Our passion is simple: build a platform that showcases the incredible work being done across the Jersey Shore. When someone visits the region or searches for a local service, we want them to find the real gems — the places locals love and newcomers discover and never forget.

Curated, not crowded

We hand-select businesses that represent genuine excellence. Every listing reflects the quality and character of the South Jersey shore.

Shore-focused, not generic

We serve three counties and three counties only. That focus is what makes us useful — for locals, for visitors, and for the businesses themselves.

Built to spotlight excellence

Our Spotlight tier exists to give the region's best businesses the premium presence they deserve — not just a name in a list, but a stage.
